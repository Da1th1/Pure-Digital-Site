FAQ

What does Pure Digital do?

Pure Digital is an app development studio in Dublin, Ireland. We design and build iOS, Android and web apps for startups, small businesses and sports organisations, from first idea to launch and beyond.

Do you only work with businesses in Dublin?

No. We are based in Dublin and work with clients across Ireland, the UK and Europe. Most projects run remotely, and we are happy to meet in person in Dublin.

Do you build apps for both iPhone and Android?

Yes. We build for iOS and Android, either natively or with a single cross-platform codebase, depending on what suits your product and budget best. We also build web apps.

How much does it cost to build an app in Ireland?

It depends on the features, platforms and integrations you need. After a free discovery call we give you a clear, itemised estimate so there are no surprises.

Can you help after the app is launched?

Yes. We offer ongoing support, maintenance, updates for new iOS and Android versions, and new feature development once your app is live.

How do I start a project with Pure Digital?

Email hello@puredigital.ie with a few lines about your idea. We will set up a free discovery call to talk through goals, timeline and budget.
